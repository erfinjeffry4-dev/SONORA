THE IDEA BEHIND SONORA

Turning visual information
into an audible experience.

SONORA uses image pixels as a creative source. Brightness, colour and position influence synthesized notes, allowing you to explore a photograph through sound. It is an artistic sonification experiment, not an AI description of the image.
